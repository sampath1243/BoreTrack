# BoreTrack Pricing & Rate Cards V4

This upgrade adds versioned rate cards. A rate card has an effective date/season and contains rate lines for drilling, Jali, casing, Freshing, Bata, transport and other charges.

Historical job charges are not recalculated when a future rate card changes.

Starter validated rates included:
- Jali ₹550/ft
- Drilling ₹70/ft
- Normal Casing ₹350/ft
- Freshing ₹45/ft
- Bata ₹1,000 (unit configurable; business validation still required)
- Transport ₹1,000 (unit configurable)
- Sudhakar is the starter preferred casing company context
